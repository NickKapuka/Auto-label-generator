Wires and Cables

The wire number should appear in the first column of the CSV. Other columns will be ignored during processing. Each wire number will be automatically duplicated to create a label for both ends of a wire.

Cable marks should appear in the first column of the cable CSV. Cable-specific formatting will be handled separately.

Empty rows and the header row will be ignored and removed during processing.
